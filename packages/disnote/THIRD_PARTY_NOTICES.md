# Third Party Notices

`@disnote/core` integrates the following third-party projects:

- BlockNote — MPL-2.0 — https://www.blocknotejs.org
- ProseMirror — MIT — https://prosemirror.net
- Yjs — MIT — https://yjs.dev
- KaTeX — MIT — https://katex.org
- MathLive — MIT — https://mathlive.io
- CortexJS Compute Engine — MIT — https://cortexjs.io/compute-engine/
- fflate — MIT — https://github.com/101arrowz/fflate

KaTeX parses stored mathematical source into semantic MathML. MathLive provides
the visual, slot-based equation input used by the React editor. No third-party
license is replaced by the Apache-2.0 license of DisNote.

- Mermaid (MIT): https://github.com/mermaid-js/mermaid — local diagram rendering.
